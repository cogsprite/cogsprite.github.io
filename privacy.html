Privacy Policy

Forgotten Atlas: Match Puzzle · Cogsprite · Last updated: 3 October 2026

Forgotten Atlas: Match Puzzle ("The Forgotten Atlas") is a single-player puzzle game made by Cogsprite, an independent game studio in Egypt ("we"). It is designed for players of all ages, including children. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

In short

  • We do not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics.
  • The game is free and shows a small number of ads from Google AdMob. All ads are requested as child-directed and non-personalized, rated for general audiences.
  • One optional purchase, "Remove ads", goes through Google Play.

What stays on your device

Your progress (pages discovered, stars, coins, boosters and settings) is saved in the game's private storage on your device. Nobody else can read it, including us. A record of whether you bought "Remove ads" is kept in a separate file on your device and is confirmed with Google Play.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show:

  • an optional short video you can choose to watch for a bonus (for example, five more moves after a loss), and
  • now and then, a short ad between pages (never during play, never in your first session).

Every ad request tells Google that the app is directed to children (under COPPA), that the user is under the age of consent (under GDPR), and that only ads rated G may be shown. With these settings Google serves contextual ads only: no personalized or interest-based ads and no remarketing.

To deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK processes limited technical information such as the device's IP address, device type and operating system, the app version, and ad interactions (for example, that an ad was shown). The game removes Android's advertising ID permission, so the advertising ID is not accessed. Google processes this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy. We use Google AdMob, which is certified for apps in the Google Play Families program.

Purchases (Google Play)

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play and tied to your Google account. We never see your payment details. Each time the game starts, it asks Google Play what your account owns, so the purchase comes back after a reinstall or on another device, and a refunded purchase is withdrawn. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase. On children's devices, Google Play's parental controls and purchase approvals apply.

Device backup

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the game's save in your device backup so your progress can be restored on a new phone. Google handles this backup under your Google account settings and Google's privacy policy. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Ads are child-directed and non-personalized as described above. Parents or guardians with questions can contact us at the address below.

Permissions

The Android app asks for internet and network-state access (to load ads and talk to Google Play) and vibration (for optional haptic feedback). The Google ads and billing libraries also declare standard permissions that need no approval: Google Play billing, basic phone state (the device type, not your phone number or calls), and keeping the device awake briefly for background tasks. The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or advertising ID.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
